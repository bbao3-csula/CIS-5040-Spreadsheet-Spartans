01 · Project Scope

Project Goal & Scope

Project Goal

Our project will help Balloons and Buttercream streamline their scheduling and booking system and provide a centralized location to record and review financial transactions. We will know we succeeded when Vibiana has a place to easily review and prepare for upcoming events, as well as look into the financial details of past events. Additionally, our group’s solution should also work for similar companies facing similar problems.

Out of Scope

To keep the project focused, our team will not:

  • Create an app.
  • Tackle advertising or outreach.

02 · Personas

The people behind every celebration

A memorable event takes clear plans, careful preparation, and a way to keep the details together. These three perspectives show what that means for Balloons and Buttercream.

A

Customer · Planning a quinceañera

Andy

A dad who wants to give his daughter a 15th birthday celebration she will always remember.

Andy is reaching out for a quote and sharing the details of his daughter’s upcoming quinceañera. He wants each custom piece to feel right for her big day.

What he hopes to arrange

  • A customized multi-layer cake with specific 15th birthday text.
  • A customized backdrop featuring traditional graphics.
  • Balloons that match the color of his daughter’s dress.

What gets in the way

  • A language barrier can make conversations with Vibiana difficult at times.
  • He only discovers later that the company cannot make the particular cake layout he had in mind.
  • The customized backdrop costs more than he expected, so he may need to reconsider it.

“I want this event to be perfect. My daughter’s quinceañera is a hugely important day for both of us.”

What success looks like

The cake, backdrop, and balloons are all made to the correct specifications.

S

Employee · Custom cake baker

Sam

The baker who makes and finishes custom cakes for the company’s events.

Sam turns each client’s requests into a finished cake, balancing creative work with a deadline tied to the event.

What they’re working toward

  • Make the cake exactly to the client’s specifications.
  • Finish it as early as possible, ideally two to three days before the event.
  • Have all the materials needed to bake and decorate it.

What gets in the way

  • The pressure to finish on time can be stressful.
  • Unclear customer details risk a cake that misses expectations and a customer who may not return.
  • Missing materials mean a shopping trip and less time to work on the cake.

“Even though it can be stressful, I honestly enjoy baking and decorating cakes. Vibiana is a really good boss, too.”

What success looks like

The customer’s wishes are clear, every material is on hand, and baking and decorating go smoothly. The cake is finished two days before the event, and the customer loves their dream cake.

V

Owner · Balloons and Buttercream

Vibiana

The owner who keeps every part of the business moving.

Vibiana manages the business while making sure customers feel cared for at every event.

What she’s working toward

  • Expand and improve the business while ensuring customer satisfaction.

What gets in the way

  • Financial bookkeeping and the current scheduling system are inefficient.
  • Without a manager to share the administrative work, many tasks fall to her.
  • She is still working out how to make the business more efficient.

“There’s a lot of pressure when I have to do everything, but this business is my life’s passion. I want to give it my all.”

What success looks like

Vibiana can easily review and prepare for upcoming events and look into the financial details of past events.

03 · Process Map

From first quote to event day

This is the current scheduling and booking journey at Balloons and Buttercream. The arrows show what happens next, where a design can be revised, and how event-day pickup or delivery leads to a final backdrop check.

Scheduling / Booking · Current process

Scroll sideways to explore the full map.

Start or endActionDecision
Read the process as text
  1. The client reaches out for a quote. Vibiana explains the pricing breakdown.
  2. Vibiana and the client discuss the design, serving size, delivery or pickup, and allergies.
  3. Vibiana sketches a concept from the client’s requests. If the client asks for changes, the sketches are revised and reviewed again.
  4. Once the client approves the sketches, preparation begins and takes at least four days.
  5. Vibiana creates the customized cake or backdrop and preserves it until event day.
  6. Most clients pick up the order. If delivery is selected, Vibiana and a staff member deliver it about three hours before the event.
  7. If the order includes a backdrop, Vibiana or a staff member recovers it after the venue event concludes. Otherwise the order is complete.

Why we mapped this process. Scheduling and booking sit at the center of the team’s project goal. Vibiana identified this workflow, along with financial recordkeeping, as an area where she wants the business to improve. A clearer view of each event also supports a central place to review upcoming work and past transactions.
