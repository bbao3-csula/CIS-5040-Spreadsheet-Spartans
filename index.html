01 · Project Scope

Project Goal & Scope

Project Goal

Our project will help Balloons and Buttercream streamline their scheduling and booking system and provide a centralized location to record and review financial transactions. We will know we succeeded when Vibiana has a place to easily review and prepare for upcoming events, as well as look into the financial details of past events. Additionally, our group’s solution should also work for similar companies facing similar problems.

Out of Scope

To keep the project focused, our team will not:

  • Create an app.
  • Tackle advertising or outreach.

06 · Working Agreements

Our commitments

  1. Participation

    Everyone should participate by sharing ideas and getting back to the team in a timely manner.

  2. Accountability

    The next part of the deadline will include check-ins to communicate the progress made.

  3. Deadlines

    It will be easier to use similar dates so as not to mix them up or forget them. Buffer time should be a day.

07 · Conflict Resolution

When challenges arise

Unresponsive teammate

Calls, emails, and texts should be sent and responded to within 72 hours.

How concerns are raised

Concerns should be brought to the group chat, as it is the fastest way to communicate.

When to involve the instructor

If there is no response in 72 hours.

08 · Success Metrics

What success means to us

What will make us proud

We want to help the business grow and have a stable, clean system running for bookings.

Behaviors that define success

We would like to show up for the team by putting in our best effort to pitch ideas, communicate concerns, and stay up to date on deadlines.
